Evidence Papers

Thermal Issues Related to Hybrid Bonding of 3D-Stacked High Bandwidth Memory: A Comprehensive Review

Review of 3D-stacked HBM thermal issues. It describes increasing heat accumulation and thermo-mechanical stress with higher stack counts, compares external cooling and hybrid-bonding approaches, and emphasizes joint thermal, mechanical, and electrical optimization. It reports substantial thermal-resistance reductions in cited hybrid-bonding studies while noting reliability trade-offs.

DOI: 10.3390/electronics14132682 · Year: 2025

Source: Electronics

C2W Hybrid Bonding Interconnect Technology for Higher Density and Better Thermal Dissipation of High Bandwidth Memory

SK hynix reports chip-to-wafer Cu/oxide hybrid bonding on multi-stack HBM real devices. The paper reports thermal resistance below 20% of conventional HBM and more than 15% stack-height reduction, while also identifying particle-induced voids, pre-bond process control, electrical failure risk, reliability, and cumulative-yield concerns.

DOI: 10.1109/ECTC51909.2023.00179 · Year: 2023

Source: 2023 IEEE ECTC

Optimization of Cu protrusion of wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding for HBM packages application

This study examines Cu protrusion and reliability in wafer-to-wafer hybrid-bonded HBM. It links CTE mismatch to thermal stress and potential device failure, shows that manufacturing and service conditions affect TSV deformation, and evaluates dielectric/process choices and debonding risk using modeling and experiments.

DOI: 10.1016/j.mssp.2022.107063 · Year: 2022

Source: Materials Science in Semiconductor Processing

Thermal and Signal Integrity Co-Design and Verification of Embedded Cooling Structure With Thermal Transmission Line for High Bandwidth Memory Module

The paper proposes an embedded cooling structure plus a thermal transmission line for an HBM module. Thermal and electromagnetic simulations show lower and more uniform temperatures together with improved evaluated eye-diagram and skew behavior. The design explicitly considers structural reliability and package dimensions.

DOI: 10.1109/TCPMT.2022.3201673 · Year: 2022

Source: IEEE Transactions on Components, Packaging and Manufacturing Technology

Heat Dissipation Improvement Using Plugged Sidewall for Three-dimensional (3D) Package of High-stacked High Bandwidth Memory (HBM)

A plugged-sidewall copper structure is proposed around a high-stacked HBM package to create an additional heat-flow path. FEA across 4-, 8-, 12-, and 16-die stacks reports more than 13% lower maximum package temperature and more than 32% lower average logic-die corner thermal stress versus conventional structures. Manufacturing feasibility and cost are not established by the reported simulation.

DOI: 10.5573/JSTS.2026.26.2.97 · Year: 2026

Source: Journal of Semiconductor Technology and Science
