Evidence Papers

Thermal Issues Related to Hybrid Bonding of 3D-Stacked High Bandwidth Memory: A Comprehensive Review

Review of thermal issues in 3D-stacked HBM. It treats heat accumulation and thermo-mechanical stress as coupled problems, compares conventional thermal approaches with hybrid bonding, and reports that hybrid-bonding design parameters can improve vertical heat transfer while introducing mechanical/reliability design constraints.

DOI: 10.3390/electronics14132682 · Year: 2025

Source: Electronics

C2W Hybrid Bonding Interconnect Technology for Higher Density and Better Thermal Dissipation of High Bandwidth Memory

C2W hybrid bonding was demonstrated on multi-stack HBM devices. The study reports strong thermal-resistance and stack-height advantages versus conventional HBM, while also describing particle/void control and process integration as reliability and yield concerns.

DOI: 10.1109/ECTC51909.2023.00179 · Year: 2023

Source: 2023 IEEE ECTC

Optimization of Cu protrusion of wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding for HBM packages application

Study of Cu protrusion in wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding for HBM. It links CTE-mismatch-induced thermal stress and manufacturing/service conditions to TSV deformation and reliability, and proposes process optimization around surface topology.

DOI: 10.1016/j.mssp.2022.107063 · Year: 2022

Source: Materials Science in Semiconductor Processing

3D Integrated Process and Hybrid Bonding of High Bandwidth Memory (HBM)

Review of HBM 3D integration and hybrid bonding. It compares W2W, D2W, and D2D bonding, discusses advantages and limitations, and covers dielectric, surface/interface, and metal-structure improvements intended to improve bonding performance and reliability.

DOI: 10.1007/s13391-025-00557-9 · Year: 2025

Source:

Thermal and Signal Integrity Co-Design and Verification of Embedded Cooling Structure With Thermal Transmission Line for High Bandwidth Memory Module

Embedded Cooling Structure with Thermal Transmission Line is evaluated for HBM using thermal and electromagnetic simulations. The approach targets lower and more uniform temperature while explicitly checking signal integrity and structural reliability rather than treating cooling in isolation.

DOI: 10.1109/TCPMT.2022.3201673 · Year: 2022

Source: IEEE Transactions on Components, Packaging and Manufacturing Technology
